Reading Journal · Books · Ideas

A reading journal of books, arguments, and ideas that stayed with me.

This section is not a list of everything I read, but a selective journal of books that left a mark on how I think. I use it as a timeline of themes, questions, and reflections that have accompanied my work and interests over time.

Less summary, more trace of thought.

How I use this page

Each entry includes the book, a short reflection, and a few ideas that stayed with me. The goal is not to review books exhaustively, but to document what they changed, clarified, or complicated.

April 2026

Group Processes: Dynamics Within and Between Groups

Rupert Brown & Sam Pehrson

A useful framework for thinking about how group identity, conflict, and social change operate across different contexts. Particularly valuable for understanding polarization as a process rather than a static condition.

  • Polarization is often sustained by group processes, not just individual attitudes.
  • Conflict sharpens boundaries and makes compromise harder to imagine.
  • Social change depends on both internal dissent and intergroup dynamics.

Twenty-Five Years of Research Using Implicit Measures

Bertram Gawronski, Jan De Houwer & Jeffrey Sherman

Helped me think more carefully about what implicit measures do and do not actually capture. It pushed me to separate methodological excitement from theoretical clarity.

  • Measurement tools are not equivalent to the constructs they aim to assess.
  • Implicit responses are informative, but often easy to overinterpret.
  • The relation between attitude and behavior is more conditional than simple.

March 2026

The Anthropology of Religion

James Bielo

A strong reminder that religion can be approached through practice, embodiment, and social life, not only through doctrine or belief. It made the category itself feel less fixed and more historically constructed.

  • Religious practice can matter more than belief alone.
  • Definitions of religion are never neutral.
  • Anthropological approaches reveal how religion is lived, not just stated.

February 2026

The Psychology of Influence

Van der Pligt & Vliek

Useful for understanding how emotions shape persuasion and behavior beyond purely rational models. A good bridge between attitude theory and practical influence.

  • Emotion is not noise added to cognition; it is part of judgment.
  • Influence often works through feeling before it works through argument.
  • Attitudes become more powerful when they connect to identity and affect.
