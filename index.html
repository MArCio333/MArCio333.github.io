Political Science · Public Policy · Internet Governance

Political analysis, public policy, and the points where domestic politics meets the wider world.

I am Marco Baltieri, a Political Science graduate based in Amsterdam, currently working in the Public Policy and Internet Governance department at RIPE NCC.

My interests sit at the intersection of domestic political division, party politics, foreign policy, and international governance, with a particular focus on Latin America.

I am especially interested in the point where domestic politics stops being domestic.
